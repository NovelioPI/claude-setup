## Commit Messages

### Subject

Write the subject in the imperative mood: "Add", "Fix", "Ignore".
Start with a capital letter.
Do not add a prefix. Do not use `feat:`, `fix:`, or a scope.
Do not end with a period.
Cap the subject at 60 characters.
Cite the issue at the end, when one applies:
`Settle the paper book on an off day (#5, #6)`.

### Body

Write a body only when the subject cannot explain the reason.
Skip the body when the subject says everything.
Write the body as bullets, not as paragraphs.
Cap each bullet at 20 words.
State why the change happened, not what the diff shows.

Risk: if the body restates the diff, then a reader gets no new fact.

### Words

Do not use a figure of speech. See @rules/plain-words.md for the full
table. Common cases: ship it, under the hood, magic, footgun, wire up,
land a change.

Keep the exact technical word. The long-word table does not apply here.

### Trailers

Put a trailer last, after one blank line.
